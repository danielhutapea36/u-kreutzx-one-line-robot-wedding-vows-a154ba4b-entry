## The line

> I promise to love you through every firmware update, every dead battery, and every reboot — and never once press factory reset.

The setup reads like a real wedding vow ("I promise to love you through..."); the punchline turns on factory reset, the one betrayal no robot forgives. Firmware updates, dead batteries and reboots are specifically robot problems, and the three-beat list builds a rhythm that lets the laugh land in the last few words.
